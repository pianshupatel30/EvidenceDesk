The centre has a bicycle rack beside the main entrance. The nearest bus stop is Harbor Square, a five-minute walk away. There is no visitor car park on the centre grounds. Small bags may be carried into the gallery. Large bags can be left in the staffed cloakroom during opening hours. The cloakroom closes at the same time as the centre. Lost property is held at reception for thirty days. Contact the visitor team with a description and the date of your visit.
